# Project Instructions & Guidelines

## Visual Identity & Design System
- **Aesthetic**: Maintain a premium, minimal, industrial visual identity.
- **Avoid SaaS Clichés**: Do not convert the website into a generic SaaS landing page.
- **Styling Constraints**: Do not use excessive gradients, oversized rounded cards, cartoon-style illustrations, or unrelated stock photography.
- **Fidelity**: Keep the design close to the supplied screenshot while adapting its content to hot dip galvanizing.

## Priorities
1. **Screenshot fidelity**
2. **Mobile usability**
3. **Clear WhatsApp conversion**
4. **Accessibility**
5. **Performance**
6. **Maintainable React architecture**
7. **Accurate business content**

## Content & Business Integrity
- **Authenticity**: Never invent business claims or customer proof.
